Internal conventions
====================

CONV-1: Time to expiry
----------------------

Time to expiry is a year fraction in calendar time, measured on the exchange's clock: (expiry − as-of) in whole minutes, divided by the minutes in 365 days, where both are exchange-local date-times without time zone, so a daylight-saving change between them is ignored. It agrees with the first data source (ivolatility), whose implied vols therefore need no conversion; a source measuring time differently has its implied vols rescaled by its adapter. It is also shown as fractional calendar days to expiry (time to expiry × 365): a one-day PM-settled weekly at the 16:15 snapshot shows 0.99 days.

CONV-2: Trading days to expiry
------------------------------

Trading days to expiry is the number of trading days after the as-of date, up to and including the settlement date. It is used where a rule is stated in trading days.

CONV-3: Settlement date, expiry and last trading day
----------------------------------------------------

The settlement date is the last trading day on or before the expiration date (it differs from the expiration date for the Saturday-dated SPX monthlies before 2015, which settled on the Friday before). The expiry is the settlement date at 09:30 ET for AM-settled roots and 16:00 ET for PM-settled roots. The last trading day is the trading day before the settlement date for AM-settled roots (normally the Thursday) and the settlement date for PM-settled roots.

CONV-4: Delta targets are absolute values
-----------------------------------------

A 25-delta put is a put whose delta is −0.25; a spec writes target: 0.25 for calls and puts alike.

CONV-5: At the money
--------------------

At the money means the 50-delta option of the leg's own type (call or put), not the strike nearest to spot. The strike nearest the series forward is a separate selector.

CONV-6: Trades execute at the triggering step
---------------------------------------------

Trades execute at the step at which the triggering condition is observed. For end-of-day data, that is the close.

CONV-7: Dollar greeks
---------------------

With q the signed quantity in contracts, m the multiplier and S the index level: dollar delta = Δ·q·m·S (equivalent index exposure); dollar gamma = Γ·q·m·S²·0.01 (change in dollar delta for a 1% index move); dollar vega = vega·q·m (per volatility point, CONV-10); dollar theta = θ·q·m (per calendar day, CONV-10).

CONV-8: Signs
-------------

A positive quantity is long; a positive cash flow is cash received.

CONV-9: A roll re-establishes the structure
-------------------------------------------

Unless the spec says otherwise, a roll closes the structure and re-establishes it using the original selection rules.

CONV-10: Prices, implied vol and greeks
---------------------------------------

Per contract: prices in index points per unit of the underlying (dollars are price × quantity × multiplier); implied volatility as a decimal, consistent with CONV-1's time to expiry; one forward per expiration and date; delta is the undiscounted Black-76 forward delta, N(d1) for a call and N(d1) − 1 for a put (for a call and a put at the same strike and implied vol, call delta − put delta = 1); gamma is per point of the forward; vega is per volatility point; theta is per calendar day.
